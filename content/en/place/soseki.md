## Where to go

227 S Dubuque St, Iowa City, IA

## What to order or shop for

Katsudon is $14: breaded pork, egg and onion over rice. Taiwanese salt-and-pepper chicken over rice is $15.55 and lists stewed egg and bok choy.

## Before heading out

The small Kakuni Don is $7.50; large is $15. The menu's Veggie Miso Ramen uses pork-bone broth, fish cake and egg, so its name does not make it vegetarian.
