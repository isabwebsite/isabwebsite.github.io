## Where to go

310 E Prentiss St, Iowa City, IA

## What to order or shop for

Lunch is 11am–2:30pm: Rachael tofu-egg-salad wrap $9.50; tempeh Reuben $10. The dinner Reuben is $16.50 with a side.

## Before heading out

Closed Sunday and Monday. Call 319-248-0077 for carryout. Some gluten-free fried items share a fryer with seitan; ask about cross-contact if that matters to you.
