## Where to go

206 N Linn St, Iowa City, IA

## What to order or shop for

Start with the Falafel Pita or Tofu Mangal Pita. For a plate, the menu lists a Grill Plate with two grill items, two sides and one pita.

## Before heading out

Open daily 11am–8pm. Prices are absent from the current official menu image; check its linked ordering menu. Ask about sauces and complete-meal ingredients for dietary needs.
