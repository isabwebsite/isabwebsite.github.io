## Where to go

320 E Burlington St, Iowa City, IA 52240

## What to order or shop for

Dandan noodles: $11.95. For a main to share, compare spicy crispy chicken ($18.95) and mapo tofu with minced pork ($16.95).

## Before heading out

Mapo tofu contains pork; it is not a vegetarian choice. The menu marks these dishes spicy. Confirm rice and portion size when planning a shared order.
