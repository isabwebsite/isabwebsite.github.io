## Where to go

53 2nd St, Coralville, IA 52241

## What to order or shop for

Beef-brisket pho is $16.95; grilled-pork bánh mì is $9.95. The sandwich lists pickled vegetables, herbs, jalapeño, pâté and mayonnaise.

## Before heading out

Vegetarian pho is $16.95, but you must select vegetable broth: chicken and beef are also offered. Closed Monday; Tuesday–Saturday 11am–9pm, Sunday 11am–8pm.
