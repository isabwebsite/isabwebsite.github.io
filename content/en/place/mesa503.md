## Where to go

2302 Muscatine Ave, Iowa City, IA 52240

## What to order or shop for

Ask for frijol y queso (beans and cheese) or queso con loroco (cheese and edible flower). Revueltas contains cheese, beans and pork.

## Before heading out

The official menu does not post prices: ask the price per pupusa and whether sides are included. The bean soup lists beef. Do not assume that a bean-based dish is meat-free.
