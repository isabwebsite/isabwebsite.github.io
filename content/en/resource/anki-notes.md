If something feels clear while you read but disappears when you close the book, try turning it into a few questions. Anki schedules card reviews using your feedback about recall.

Start with ten cards from material you understand but do not yet remember easily.

## One clear question

A vocabulary card might ask what a word means in a particular sentence. Add a short explanation and example on the back. If an answer needs three paragraphs, split it up or keep it in your regular notes.

## Recall before revealing

Try an answer before turning the card over. Judge what you recalled, rather than how familiar the answer looks afterward.

## Give it a small space in your day

Try ten minutes a day for a few days. If reviews pile up, reduce new cards before adding more. Treat the schedule as an experiment, not a scorecard.

## Check the platform before downloading

Desktop Anki and AnkiWeb syncing are free. AnkiDroid is free on Android; AnkiMobile is paid on iOS. Follow the project’s own download links to avoid confusing similarly named apps.
