A download folder can hold the article you need without making it easy to find. Try keeping the source and your own reading notes beside each paper.

Zotero collects and organizes references and helps with citations. You do not need to learn every feature to begin.

## Start with three papers

Make a collection for a specific question, such as “next discussion: sleep and memory.” Add three papers you are already reading. Check their titles, authors and years, including information imported automatically.

## Leave a sentence for your future self

Try a note like “explains the method I was missing” or “compare this conclusion with the other paper.” These are starting points for reading, not substitutes for checking the original before citing it.

## Decide whether you need syncing

Local use is separate from cloud storage. Reference data can sync for free; attachment storage, including PDFs, has a free allowance and paid upgrades. Decide whether you need the files on several devices before paying for more space.

## Leave a way back in

Finish with one question you have not answered yet. It gives your next reading session somewhere to begin.
