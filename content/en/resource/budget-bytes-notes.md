Groceries can all seem useful in the shop, then feel like unrelated ingredients at home. Choose one or two things you want to use tonight and look for a recipe around them.

Budget Bytes has categories including one-pot meals, vegetarian dishes and quick recipes. Its estimated costs are comparison clues, not live prices at your local store.

## Check what the meal asks of you

Look past the finished photo. Check the pan, time and seasonings needed. For a first attempt, avoid buying many ingredients you are unlikely to use again.

## Count the servings

Think about dinner and leftovers before deciding how much to prepare. Your pan and fridge space are part of the plan too.

## Shop only for what is missing

Compare the ingredients with what you already have. Everyday staples can carry into another meal; one recipe does not need to become a whole kitchen restock.

Heading out for groceries? Read the [ALDI note](/en/guides/practical/aldi-shopping-notes/) for bags and the trip home.

You can start with public recipes. Weekly meal plans and an ad-free experience are paid features; decide later whether you need them.
