Iowa winters can feel much colder when wind increases. Dress in loose layers and cover your head, ears and hands. A winter coat, snow boots and gloves make everyday campus trips more comfortable.

## Before you go

- Check the forecast and wind chill before leaving.
- Wear layers, a warm coat, hat and gloves.
- Choose water-resistant boots with traction; keep spare gloves in your bag.
