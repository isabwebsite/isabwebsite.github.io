Maquoketa Caves State Park has cave-linked trails and scenic overlooks. Caves close annually from November 15 to April 1 for overwintering bats. Trails include rocky terrain, steep inclines and stairs. Approximate drive from central Iowa City: 1.5 hours; verify current routing.

## Before you go

- Check Iowa DNR's live park alerts and seasonal cave closure.
- Wear suitable walking footwear and bring a light for cave exploration.
- Choose trails appropriate to your mobility and experience.
- Check weather and road conditions before departure.
