Explore the Amana Colonies' seven historic villages, local shops and food traditions. A 14-mile loop connects the villages. Approximate drive from central Iowa City: 35–45 minutes; verify current routing.

## Before you go

- Use the visitors bureau's current itinerary or event calendar.
- Choose one or two villages if you want a relaxed half-day.
- Check business and museum hours before leaving.
