Czech Village combines shops, murals and food with the National Czech & Slovak Museum & Library. It offers a close city outing and a chance to explore immigrant history. Approximate drive from central Iowa City: 35–45 minutes; verify current routing.

## Before you go

- Check the museum's admission and current hours.
- Pair a museum visit with a walk through the neighborhood.
- Leave time for a café or meal.
