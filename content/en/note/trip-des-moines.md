Visit the Des Moines Art Center and explore a city itinerary from the official tourism bureau. Its sculpture park can pair well with a downtown walk. Approximate drive from central Iowa City: 2–2.5 hours; verify current routing and allow a full day.

## Before you go

- Check the Art Center's current hours and visitor information.
- Choose one museum plus one nearby neighborhood or outdoor stop.
- Plan breaks and a return drive before dark in winter.
