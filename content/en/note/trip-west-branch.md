A nearby history outing to Herbert Hoover National Historic Site in West Branch. Pick up a visitor map and explore the historic area. The NPS visitor center is off I-80 exit 254. Approximate drive from central Iowa City: 20–30 minutes; verify current routing.

## Before you go

- Check NPS hours and any alerts.
- Start at 110 Parkside Drive.
- Distinguish the National Historic Site from the separate Presidential Library and Museum when checking tickets and access.
