Galena's historic Main Street offers shops and restaurants in an 1800s streetscape. A self-guided walking tour explores its history and architecture. Approximate drive from central Iowa City: 2 hours; verify current routing.

## Before you go

- Choose a walking route from the official visitor guide.
- Wear comfortable shoes; some historic routes use steep terrain.
- Check any house-tour or museum tickets separately.
