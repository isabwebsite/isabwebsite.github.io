## Plan the trip

1. Install Transit and enter your destination; set the actual travel date and time.
2. Check that the trip says Iowa City Transit, then open the walking directions to the boarding stop.
3. At the signed stop, match the bus route number and direction to your trip. Board without buying an Iowa City Transit ticket.
4. Save your return trip and check service alerts before leaving.

## A few more things

- The city's service is free to the general public; the fare does not depend on a student card.
- Predicted arrivals are estimates. Check the live map and allow extra time when the trip matters.
