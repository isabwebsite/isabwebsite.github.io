## Plan the trip

1. Save your complete accommodation address and the host's check-in instructions offline.
2. For a prebooked option, request Airport Express online or call 319-626-5468. Wait for the emailed confirmation and pickup timing; a request alone is not a confirmed ride.
3. Airport Express lists CID pickup at Baggage Claim Door E. Reconfirm the meeting point with your booking.
4. If using an app instead, CID lists Uber and Lyft as airport pickup options. Check the live fare, vehicle capacity, and pickup instructions after collecting luggage.

## A few more things

- Ask the operator how it handles a delayed flight and how many bags are included before paying.
- Use the checkout total for your budget: shuttle taxes and driver gratuity are not included in the displayed base amount. Availability and app fares vary.
