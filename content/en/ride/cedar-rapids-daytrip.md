## Plan the trip

1. Check the Corridor Loop schedule in both directions before choosing your appointment time.
2. Downtown Iowa City boarding is at Court Street Transportation Center, 170 E. Court Street. Coralville boarding is at Intermodal, 906 Quarry Road.
3. For downtown Cedar Rapids, use Ground Transportation Center, 450 1st Street SE; follow the operator's stop-location directions.
4. Pay exact cash or buy a ticket in Token Transit. Activate a mobile ticket only when ready to board; it expires 30 minutes after activation.

## A few more things

- Saturday service is currently not operating; this is a weekday plan.
- This route does not list CID airport among its stops. For a flight, use a separately planned airport connection.
- ISAB guide tip: plan your trip around the return bus first, especially for a late appointment.
