## Plan the trip

1. Check the operator shown for every leg of your journey.
2. Bring exact cash for Coralville: drivers do not give change. Ask for a $2 24-hour pass before putting money in the farebox if that suits your day.
3. If you need two different Coralville routes for one onward trip, ask the driver for a transfer when paying.
4. When changing to Iowa City Transit, simply board; its fare-free service needs no transfer ticket.

## A few more things

- A transfer continues one one-way trip; it is not a return ticket and cannot be used on the same Coralville route.
- A $2 pass costs the same as two $1 rides; consider it for flexibility or additional trips.
