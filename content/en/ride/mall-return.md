## Plan the trip

1. Search the complete outbound and return trip in Transit for the actual day. Confirm that your selected bus serves your starting stop.
2. At the mall, locate stop #4220 on the south side near the east end, close to the movie-theater entrance.
3. Before entering a shop or movie, set a reminder before your chosen return departure and allow time to reach that stop.
4. Recheck the bus direction and alerts when you leave.

## A few more things

- Night service has a last bus. Check the last departure at your own stop rather than relying on systemwide operating hours.
- Large purchases must fit in your personal space and keep the aisle clear; plan another way home for bulky move-in items.
