## Plan the trip

1. For a Sunday appointment or shopping plan, set the journey planner to Sunday before deciding how to travel.
2. ISAB guide tip: move a big grocery run to Saturday when a bus is important to your plan.
3. If travelling Sunday, compare a walkable destination, a ride with a friend, or a taxi/rideshare; check both directions and the live price.

## A few more things

- A shop being open does not mean its weekday bus route is running.
- Holidays can also change service. Check the operator's alerts for your travel date.
