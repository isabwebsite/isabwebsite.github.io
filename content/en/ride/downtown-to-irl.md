## Plan the trip

1. In Transit, search Downtown Interchange → Intermodal and select the date of your trip.
2. Look for Coralville 20 — Iowa River Landing, then follow the app to the correct boarding stop and check the destination direction.
3. Get off at Intermodal, 906 Quarry Road, and use walking directions for your final shop or restaurant.
4. Before shopping, check Intermodal → Downtown Interchange for your return time.

## A few more things

- Route 20 is listed for Monday–Friday. For an evening or Saturday trip, plan again; routes 24 and 25 use different patterns.
- ISAB guide tip: keep the first shopping trip small enough to carry comfortably on the bus.
