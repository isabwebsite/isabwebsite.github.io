## Dates and places for this edition

2026: Saturdays, May 2–October 31 · 7:30 a.m.–noon

415 E. Washington St. · Chauncey Swan parking ramp, park and adjoining street.

## An idea for your visit

Our suggestion: take a reusable bag, buy breakfast and a few vegetables, then stay for Market Music, scheduled 9 a.m.–noon. Payment options vary by vendor, so ask before ordering.

For 2026, upper ramp levels and the lot behind Robert A. Lee Recreation Center have free parking during market hours. Pets can visit the park and street, but not the ramp’s first level. Check the city page before a trip; 2027 dates are not listed here.
