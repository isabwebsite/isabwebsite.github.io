## Dates and places for this edition

Summer · July 3–5, 2026 has passed

Downtown Iowa City · stages and booths around Clinton Street and Iowa Avenue; check the festival map for the next edition.

## An idea for your visit

ISAB’s suggestion: choose a set from the program, bring a blanket or folding chair, and make an evening of music and downtown dinner. You can attend a single set without committing to the whole weekend.

The confirmed 2026 edition ran July 3–5. As of October 17, no 2027 date was found on the organizer pages checked. Use the organizer’s next program for dates, stage locations and weather updates.
